About

My name is Damla Zeynep Sahin. I am 19 years old and currently studying at Vilnius Gediminas Technical University (VILNIUS TECH). Previously, I attended Yarmouth Consolidated Memorial High School in Nova Scotia, Canada, where I strengthened my communication skills in multicultural settings. I work as a Turkish–English interpreter at DALS, supporting clear and accurate communication across languages. I also served as Marketing and Communications Director at Alataki Jewelry, where I managed online sales, coordinated the website, and engaged customers. I enjoy building practical digital products—one of my recent projects is a Reminder App that brings birthdays, important dates, and custom categories together with optional alarms.
